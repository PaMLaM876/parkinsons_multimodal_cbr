🎓 Presentation Guide for Your Professor (What to Highlight in Review 1)

1. Speech Biomarker Preprocessing (CNN + BiLSTM)

Explain that speech recordings undergo Voice Activity Detection (VAD) and Short-Time Fourier Transform (STFT) onto 64 Mel filterbanks. This creates a time-frequency tensor (1, 64, 184) allowing 2D Conv layers to capture formant shifts and BiLSTM layers to track temporal phonatory micro-tremors (5 Hz modulation) and acoustic jitter.

2. 3D MRI Structural Neuroimaging (3D ResNet-50)

Highlight that raw PPMI scans have varying resolutions and non-brain tissue. Our automated 3D Otsu & morphological pipeline skull-strips the brain volume, resamples it isometrically to (96, 96, 96), and applies 1st–99th percentile Z-score standardization to eliminate scanner bias artifacts.

3. PPMI Clinical & Dopaminergic Integration

Show that clinical scores (MDS-UPDRS Parts I–IV, Hoehn & Yahr, MoCA) are paired with DaTscan SPECT Striatal Binding Ratios (SBR). The preprocessor robustly imputes missing values and encodes categorical variables into a clean 37-dimensional vector.

4. Multimodal Synchronization & CBR Readiness

Demonstrate that the PyTorch MultimodalDataset synchronizes all three modalities per patient ID, provides binary modality presence masks for missing data, and creates stratified train/val/test splits ready for Multimodal Fusion and Case-Based Reasoning.
